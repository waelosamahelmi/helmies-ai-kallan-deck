Your Slack-based team structure

A simple operating system for distributed work

The strongest version of this setup is not “AI in Slack” as a gimmick. It is a clean structure where Slack becomes the intake, coordination, and memory layer for the team.

Intake

Every request lands in the right channel with context, owner, deadline, and priority instead of living in scattered DMs.

Triage

Helmies AI summarizes the request, identifies the next action, and routes it to the right person or project thread.

Follow-through

It turns conversation into a short brief, checklist, and status update so work does not disappear after the meeting.

Recommended channel structure

  • #founder-ops — priorities, decisions, admin, reminders
  • #client-projects — project-specific execution and updates
  • #creative-review — feedback, iteration, approvals
  • #research-intel — sources, notes, summaries, findings

How the AI helps inside Slack

  • Drafts replies and status notes in your tone
  • Creates summaries after long threads
  • Extracts action items and owners
  • Repackages chaos into a clear plan
